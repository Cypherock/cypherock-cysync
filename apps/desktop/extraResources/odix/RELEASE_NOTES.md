# v2.4.0

### 🚀 Features

- New look, fresh feel: Odix has a fresh new look that's both modern and easy on the eyes
- Smooth moves: New animations and transitions make Odix feel more responsive and fluid
- Errors are a thing of the past: New error messages help you troubleshoot problems quickly and easily
- Say goodbye to manual wallet sync with Odix's new automatic sync feature
- Add all your existing accounts and create new accounts on a chain to Odix with just a few taps. It's the fastest and easiest way to manage your crypto
- Odix now connects to your Odix Vault more reliably and quickly, thanks to an improved communication API
- We've squashed some pesky bugs in Odix, so you can enjoy a smoother experience
